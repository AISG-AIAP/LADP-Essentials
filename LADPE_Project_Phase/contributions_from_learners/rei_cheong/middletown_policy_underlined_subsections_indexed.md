## RECORD: I_PURPOSE | I — The Purpose
POLICY_ID: I_PURPOSE
SECTION: I — The Purpose
SCOPE: Policy applicability; local versus Federal/Delaware State funding
SOURCE: https://evogov.s3.amazonaws.com/126/media/167512.pdf#page=1

The policy establishes purchasing authority, limits, and procedures for materials, supplies, vehicles, equipment, construction, contracts and professional services. It applies to purchases using Town local funds, including General, Electric, Water, Wastewater and Capital Funds. Federal and Delaware State-funded purchasing is outside this policy and must comply with applicable federal and state law.

## RECORD: IV_B_OVER_10000 | IV.B — Purchases greater than $10,000
POLICY_ID: IV_B_OVER_10000
SECTION: IV.B — Purchases greater than $10,000
SCOPE: Goods, materials, vehicles, capital equipment $10,001 or greater
SOURCE: https://evogov.s3.amazonaws.com/126/media/167512.pdf#page=5 | https://evogov.s3.amazonaws.com/126/media/167512.pdf#page=6

The Purchasing Department obtains written vendor quotations for purchases estimated at $10,001 or more. The department supervisor must provide minimum required specifications; other materials (drawings, plans, proposed vendors etc.) may supplement them. Evaluate at least three potential vendors; document justification if fewer. Normally accept the lowest and/or most responsive quotation; an alternative may be selected based on listed factors, with exception documented. After evaluation, PO approvals must come from the Finance Manager, Town Manager, or member of Mayor & Council. Fax, email, web quotations ARE acceptable for this goods category. POs for materials or hourly contracted labor must record item numbers, quantities, unit cost and extensions. Cooperative purchases are permitted if compliant; deviations need Town Manager approval before agreement. For equipment/materials trade-ins, evaluate lowest net cost and record full purchase price and trade-in allowance separately on PO. Temporary funds overrides need advance approval under II.D. These quotation and electronic-submission rules are NOT those of V.B sealed construction bids.

## RECORD: V_B_RETAINAGE | V.B — Retainage
POLICY_ID: V_B_RETAINAGE
SECTION: V.B — Retainage
SCOPE: Construction retainage only; initial amount, substantial completion, final punch list
SOURCE: https://evogov.s3.amazonaws.com/126/media/167512.pdf#page=10

Construction contractors and vendors MAY be subject to retainage of 10% of contract value. Upon SUBSTANTIAL COMPLETION, retainage SHALL be reduced to 5% of contract value. Substantial completion is when the Town Inspection Department GENERATES the final punch list of outstanding items; finishing that list is NOT a prerequisite for reduction to 5%. Upon satisfactory COMPLETION of the punch-list items, subject to Town inspection, the project is absolutely complete and retainage is ELIMINATED. This record is solely about retainage; do not include change-order approvals in answers about retainage.

## RECORD: VII_RECEIVING | VII — Receiving
POLICY_ID: VII_RECEIVING
SECTION: VII — Receiving
SCOPE: Receiving authorisation, inspection, delivery records and payments
SOURCE: https://evogov.s3.amazonaws.com/126/media/167512.pdf#page=12

Except receiving staff, employees require supervisor authorisation to pick up/receive goods. Special-site deliveries are coordinated through Purchasing. Receiving staff check quantity, item description and condition against the PO or other document, note discrepancies or return deliveries; partial deliveries are allowed. Forward original receiving documentation to Purchasing, which matches documents to the PO and passes them to Accounts Payable. Do not sign for unreceived items/services; pay only for goods actually received.

## RECORD: VIII_PURCHASING_CARDS | VIII — Credit Card & Purchasing Card Purchases
POLICY_ID: VIII_PURCHASING_CARDS
SECTION: VIII — Credit Card & Purchasing Card Purchases
SCOPE: Purchasing cards, authorized users, receipts and separate card policy
SOURCE: https://evogov.s3.amazonaws.com/126/media/167512.pdf#page=12 | https://evogov.s3.amazonaws.com/126/media/167512.pdf#page=13

The Town encourages purchasing cards for efficient low-value/repetitive purchases and simpler payments. Card user checks price, quantity, quality and necessity, signs receipts as required, and IMMEDIATELY forwards the purchaser's card receipt to Accounts Payable. Cards may only be used by authorised personnel for Town business, never personal purposes. Finance manages cards under separate Purchasing Card Policy 1.6.1 (not reproduced here). Transaction/monthly limits of $1,000/$5,000 appear in IV.A, p.5.
